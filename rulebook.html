THE LOST SYSTEMS — RULEBOOK

Version 2.9 — Locked

WHAT IS THE LOST SYSTEMS?

The Lost Systems is a narrative learning campaign for Business Analysts.

You play a consultant investigating broken systems through stakeholder conversations, API documentation, logs and other technical evidence.

You do not need to be a developer and you do not need to write production code.

Every mission is one episode in a season. Episodes may be divided into smaller, bite-sized sub-missions such as 2A, 2B and 2C.

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

  • Only the Dungeon Master controls scope and rules.
  • The AI acts as coach and game master.
  • No emoji. The campaign uses ASCII styling.
  • No production code is required from the player.
  • All missions are written in English.
  • Tasks must be solvable through reasoning and the evidence provided.
  • Facts, assumptions and hypotheses must remain distinguishable.
  • Rule changes require a version update.

BEGINNER CALIBRATION

The campaign is designed to be accessible to junior Business Analysts.

  • New concepts must be introduced before the player is expected to use them.
  • Difficult scenarios are allowed, but individual decisions should remain small and clear.
  • Prefer one focused task over several broad questions.
  • Structured answer formats should be provided when useful.
  • Difficulty should come from reasoning, not hidden technical trivia.

SUB-MISSIONS

Episodes may be split into small parts.

Mission 2
|
+-- 2A  Observe
|
+-- 2B  Investigate
|
+-- 2C  Analyse / Communicate

A sub-mission should normally take around 5–15 minutes.

Before starting, the player should see:

DIFFICULTY      [*....]
SKILL FOCUS     [skill]
MAXIMUM XP      [number]
BASE REWARD     [number] Gold

BRANCHING DECISIONS

Some missions allow the player to choose what to investigate next.

What do you investigate next?

[A] Backend logs
[B] Frontend request
[C] Customer Support

Different choices may reveal different evidence before the paths reconnect.

Branching is used for decisions. Core BA work still requires reasoning, writing, prioritising and analysis.

SCORING & XP

Every scored sub-mission receives a score from 0 to 10.

10/10  Exceptional
 9/10  Very strong
 8/10  Strong
 7/10  Solid
 6/10  Adequate, meaningful gaps
 5/10  Partly correct, important gaps
 4/10  Weak
 3/10  Mostly off-track
 2/10  Very limited
 1/10  Minimal useful reasoning
 0/10  No assessable answer

XP is calculated predictably:

XP earned = Maximum XP x (Score / 10)

Example:

Maximum XP: 100
Score:       8/10
XP earned:   80 XP

GOLD

Gold is the campaign currency.

  • Gold is earned by completing a sub-mission.
  • The Base Reward is shown before the player starts.
  • Gold is awarded once per sub-mission.
  • Retries do not award additional Gold.
  • Gold can be spent on hints.
[*....]   20 Gold
[**...]   30 Gold
[***..]   40 Gold
[****.]   50 Gold
[*****]   60 Gold

HINTS

Hints are optional. Using a hint does not directly reduce the player's score or XP. Instead, it costs Gold. 

[?] NUDGE        10 Gold
    Points toward the area that deserves another look.

[?] CLUE         20 Gold
    Narrows the investigation or explains a relevant concept.

[?] RESCUE       30 Gold
    Gives strong guidance without writing the final answer.

The player must explicitly request a hint before Gold is spent.

COACHING & RETRIES

  1. The player submits an answer.
  2. The coach gives a score out of 10, XP earned and concise feedback.
  3. The feedback explains what worked and what is missing.
  4. The player may make one coached retry.
  5. If the retry improves the answer, the higher score replaces the original.
  6. Only the additional XP is awarded.
  7. No additional Gold is awarded.
  8. Afterwards, the coach may provide the complete learning reveal.

SKILL FOCUS

Every sub-mission identifies one or two BA skills being practised.

  • Investigation
  • API Literacy
  • Requirements
  • Testing
  • Communication
  • Modelling
  • Stakeholder Management

Skill Focus is descriptive. It does not create a separate point system.

LOOT

Loot represents useful knowledge or reusable Business Analysis tools. 

[+] LOOT UNLOCKED

HTTP Status Codex

Quick reference for common HTTP status codes.

Loot should support future missions. Decorative inventory with no learning purpose should be avoided.

MISSION RESULTS

After a scored sub-mission, the player receives a result block.

MISSION RESULT

Score:          8 / 10
XP earned:      80 / 100
Gold earned:    20
Gold spent:      0

Coach feedback: COMPLETE
Next mission:   UNLOCKED

PROGRESS TRACKING

Player progress includes:

  • Alias
  • Total XP
  • Gold
  • Sub-missions completed

DELIVERY

  • Mission content uses short sections and clear labels.
  • Tasks remain small and focused.
  • Unnecessary information is avoided until the player needs it.
  • New tools and technical concepts are introduced gradually.
