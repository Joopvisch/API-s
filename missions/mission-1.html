▓▓▓ INTRO ▓▓▓

It is your first week at Kester Digital. You are still learning names, systems and where the coffee machine is, when a message appears in the team chat.

Several users have been logged out of the mobile app without warning. The reports seem random. Some users say it happens after a few minutes. Others say it happens after returning to the app later in the day.

Three teams have already shared their first opinion.

Backend: “The token is valid when we issue it.”

Mobile: “We refresh tokens when needed.”

Support: “Users say they are suddenly logged out.”

Your lead turns to you.

“Before we start blaming each other, I want to know whether we’re asking the right questions.”
